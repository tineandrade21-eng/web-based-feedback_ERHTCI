Features

Everything you need to be heard

A simple, organized channel between students and the school administration.

Easy submission

Choose a category, add a subject and write your message in under a minute.

Anonymous or named

Send feedback without your name, or show it when you want a personal reply.

Organized categories

Teachers, facilities, academic services, student services, activities and other concerns.

Track your feedback

See whether each submission is new, pending, reviewed or resolved.

Admin dashboard

Administrators see totals at a glance and manage every submission in one place.

Search and filter

Find feedback by keyword, category or status to focus on what matters most.
